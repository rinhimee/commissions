Need harms? I’m your girl ♪

Hi, I'm rin, aka rin姫! I've been recording covers online since 2020, and I create harmony guides and original vocal arrangements for youtaites, cover singers, and VTubers.

I'm a huge, long-time choir and a cappella nerd, and I really love how harms and adlibs can completely change the sound of a song.

What’s the difference?

Each layer is recorded separately, so you can easily follow and sing along to the guides without having to pick them apart yourself.

Harmony guide (1:1) I transcribe the original song’s harmonies as close to exactly as possible.

Vocal arrangement I arrange original harmonies and/or adlibs, built around your voice, so that your cover is extra special and one-of-a-kind.

Pricing

Price quotes are non-committal and are always provided before payment, free of charge, so feel free to reach out at any time.

Please note that the prices above are minimum base prices for 1-2 simple harmony/adlib tracks. The final quote will increase based on length and complexity.

Main melody guide

An optional lead vocal track so you can hear where the harmonies sit against the melody. Please note that if you pick partial but the song has harmonies running through basically the whole thing, I’ll let you know and quote it as a full melody guide instead.
